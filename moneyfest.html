INSIDE MONEYFEST

MoneyFest is exactly what the name promises — a celebration of hustle, art and unapologetic flex. Wrapped end-to-end in bold graffiti from Kenya's top street artists, every panel is a billboard for Nairobi's youth movement.

Inside, premium interiors and a heavy sound rig turn the daily commute into an event. When MoneyFest pulls up, the whole block knows.

It's one of the most photographed nganyas in the fleet — a favourite for brand activations, shoots and anyone who wants their arrival to be the main event.

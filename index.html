The Movement

MORE THAN
TRANSPORT.
IT'S CULTURE.

RaptorFam KE is where Nairobi's streets come alive. We're not moving people — we're moving culture. From the graffiti murals to the DJ booths, from the LED strips to the premium interiors, every nganya in our fleet is a billboard for Kenya's most vibrant youth movement.

Our fleet — Mood, MoneyFest, Matrix, Baba Yaga, and Raptor — are fan favorites recognized across Africa. Mood's launch at KICC brought thousands of young Kenyans to the streets. Baba Yaga won Crew of the Year at the Nganya Awards 2025. This is not just transport.

Each vehicle represents a generation of young creatives: painters, fabricators, DJs, sound engineers, and street culture enthusiasts who built something legendary from Nairobi's streets.

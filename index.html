The Movement

MORE THAN
TRANSPORT.
IT'S CULTURE.

RaptorFam KE is where Nairobi's streets come alive. We're not moving people — we're moving culture. From the graffiti murals to the DJ booths, from the LED strips to the premium interiors, every nganya in our fleet is a billboard for Kenya's most vibrant youth movement.

Our fleet — Mood, MoneyFest, Matrix, Baba Yaga, and Raptor — are fan favorites recognized across Africa. Each one represents a generation of young creatives: painters, fabricators, DJs, sound engineers, and street culture enthusiasts who built something legendary.

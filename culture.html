The Movement

MORE THAN
TRANSPORT.

RaptorFam KE started in 2015 as a vision: to transform Nairobi's matatu culture into a global art movement. What began with one custom-built nganya (Raptor) has grown into a fleet of 5 iconic vehicles, each a masterpiece of Kenyan street art.

We're not just moving people — we're moving culture. From graffiti murals to DJ booths, every RaptorFam vehicle is a billboard for Kenya's most vibrant youth movement.

The Journey

TIMELINE

2015
Raptor — the first nganya — hits Nairobi streets
2018
Mood joins the fleet with full DJ booth and LED system
2020
MoneyFest and Matrix debut. RaptorFam goes viral on TikTok
2023
Baba Yaga completes the "Big Five." International media coverage
2025
Africa-wide recognition. 100K+ community members
